About me

I am Abdullah, from Bangladesh and now living in Larnaca, Cyprus. I started building WordPress websites in 2019 and have launched live news portals since then.

Along the way I designed for a travel brand, a food and beverage company and a clothing brand, edited and published a monthly literary magazine, ran two food carts and founded a youth-led social organization. I like work where I can make something, put it online and see people use it.

Today I study a Foundation Programme at UCLan Cyprus and work part-time as a Wolt courier, which keeps me punctual and comfortable speaking English every day. I am now looking for a full-time social media and WordPress role where I can keep a brand's pages active and up to date.
